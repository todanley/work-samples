I write and design for small businesses — shops, product lines, and short videos. The work below is original. I made each piece end to end, as a sample, so you can see the standard I work to.

They are not client projects. I would rather show you real writing than invent a past I don’t have. If you want something closer to your brief, I can do a short unpaid sample on a page or two of yours.

Demonstration brand

Tidewell

A fictional Australian skincare line. Three products, written and designed for a climate that runs from office air-conditioning to a windy foreshore. Homepage, product pages, a journal essay, emails, Instagram, Meta ads, and a thirty-second Reel script — one voice, carried all the way through.

“Made for the kind of day that starts in air-conditioning and ends in a nor’easter.”

The Small Holding

A fictional regional bottle shop. A drier, shorter voice than Tidewell — homepage, tasting notes, and a weekly email. Written for people who will tell you what they’re cooking, and how much they actually want to spend.
